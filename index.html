ViralCut turns long live streams into short vertical clips.

ViralCut is a self-hosted application. It finds the strongest moments in a stream recording, cuts them into 9:16 clips with captions, writes a title for each clip and, when its owner chooses, uploads the clip as a Short to a YouTube channel the owner manages.

Who uses it. ViralCut runs on its owner's own computer. It is operated by Somil Athole for two YouTube channels he owns and manages: Stream Kaand (Hindi streams, Indian audience) and Stream Frenzy (English streams). It is not offered as a service to other people.

What it does

  1. Records or downloads a public stream the owner chooses to clip.
  2. Transcribes the speech and finds moments where the audio and live chat spike.
  3. Cuts each moment into a vertical clip with burned-in captions.
  4. Writes a title, description and hashtags for each clip. The owner can edit or delete any clip before it is posted.
  5. Uploads chosen clips to the owner's own channel through the YouTube Data API, spaced out and capped per day.

How it uses YouTube API Services

ScopeUsed for
youtube.uploadUploading a finished clip as a Short to the owner's channel (videos.insert).
youtube.readonlyReading the connected channel's name, and the view count of videos ViralCut itself uploaded (videos.list), so the owner can see which clips performed.

ViralCut signs in with Google's own OAuth page; it never sees a password. The owner can disconnect a channel in ViralCut at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
