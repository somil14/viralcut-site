Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what ViralCut does with information it gets from YouTube API Services and Google sign-in. ViralCut is a self-hosted application operated by Somil Athole ("we") for YouTube channels he owns and manages.

ViralCut uses YouTube API Services

ViralCut uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and read basic channel and video information. By using ViralCut you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

InformationWhy
The connected channel's ID and nameTo show which channel a clip will be posted to
OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by GoogleTo upload to that channel without asking for a password
IDs and view counts of videos ViralCut uploadedTo show the owner how each posted clip is performing

ViralCut does not read comments, subscribers, private videos, messages, payment data or any other user's data. It does not use YouTube data for advertising, and it does not sell or share it with anyone.

How information is stored

  • Everything is stored on the owner's own computer, in ViralCut's local data folder. No copy is kept on any other server.
  • OAuth tokens are stored in a local file readable only by the account that runs ViralCut.
  • View counts are refreshed every 30 minutes for the first 14 days after a clip is posted, and deleted once they are 30 days old without a refresh.
  • The AI service that writes titles receives only the words spoken in a clip and the stream title, never YouTube account data or tokens.

Deleting information and revoking access

  • Press Disconnect next to a channel in ViralCut: its tokens and stored channel details are deleted at once.
  • Revoke ViralCut's access at any time at Google security settings: Third-party apps with account access.
  • To ask for deletion of any information, email sathole2001@gmail.com. We delete it within 7 days.

Cookies

ViralCut's local dashboard uses a session cookie on the owner's own computer. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes.
